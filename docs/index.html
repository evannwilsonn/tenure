      Where to draw the line

      The model only gives a probability. Someone still has to decide how high is high enough to pick up the phone. Lower the line and you catch more leavers, but you also call more people who were staying anyway. Everything here is measured on the 1,409 test customers.

      Why not accuracy

      About 27% of these customers leave. A model that says nobody leaves is 73% accurate and catches no one. F1 weighs precision and recall together, so the line ships where F1 peaked in cross-validation instead of at 50%.

      What moved the score

      At a 50% line the model caught about half the leavers (F1 ). Dropping the line to did most of the work. Weighting churners more heavily did something similar, but less well. Log-tenure, a count of add-ons and a "first six months" flag helped a little.

      Why not trees

      ML.NET's gradient-boosted trees, with their own tuned line, reached F1 on the test set. That's close, but lower, and you can't read a reason off a tree the way you can off a coefficient.
